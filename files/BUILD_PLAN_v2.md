# LUMON_TERMINAL — v2 build plan

Supersedes sections 9, 10, 17, 18, 20, 30 of the original specification.
Everything here is generated by `lumon_v2.py`; change a parameter, rerun, get
new STEP/STL/drawings.

**Envelope: 359 × 153 × 144 mm** (W × D × H)

---

## 1. Why the numbers moved

| | blockout | v2 | reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| width | 353 | 359 | spine wall thickened to 9 mm so inserts sit inside it |
| height | 130 | 144 | 18 mm plinth: the display needs ≥12 mm of cavity above and below for M3 bosses. At 130 there were 6 mm and no way to clamp the panel |
| depth | 153 | 153 | unchanged |

The original 340–350 × 135–145 targets were guesses. 359 is what the real
components plus 3 mm walls actually produce. Do not compress it back.

---

## 2. Split architecture

One seam, vertical, on the spine between display and printer zones. It is
forced by the bed (359 mm fits nothing) and it is the only seam that also
reads as deliberate industrial detailing.

```
        tub_left  (201)              tub_right  (158)
   ┌──────────────────────────┬──┬──────────────────────┐
   │  display + electronics   │  │   printer well       │
   │  open top → top_plate    │  │   open top, no lid   │
   └──────────────────────────┴──┴──────────────────────┘
                          3 mm  9 mm
                          wall  spine wall
```

Each module is a **monocoque tub**: floor, four walls and front face printed
as one piece, open at the top. Tubs are stiff, have few tolerance stacks, and
both print in the same orientation so the front-face layer lines line up
across the seam.

### Parts

| part | mm (X×Y×Z) | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `tub_left` | 201 × 153 × 144 | display window, top rebate, retainer bosses, rear opening |
| `tub_right` | 158 × 153 × 144 | printer well open to the top face, printer riser rails, 9 mm spine wall |
| `top_plate_left` | 197 × 149 × 3 | drops flush into the rebate, 4 × M3 |
| `display_retainer` | 194 × 4 × 139 | clamps the panel against the front wall lip |
| `rear_panel_left` | 185 × 6 × 120 | service access, 4 × M3 |
| `rear_panel_right` | 110 × 6 × 90 | service access, 4 × M3 |

All six fit a 250 × 210 bed. Nothing needs diagonal placement.

### Seam joint

- 5 × M3 socket head through the 3 mm wall of `tub_left` into heat-set inserts
  **sunk into the 9 mm spine wall** of `tub_right`. No boss protrudes, which
  matters because the printer sits 2 mm from that wall.
- 2 × Ø4 steel dowels (hole Ø4.3) locate the seam before the screws go in.
- Screw heads are reached from inside the left module with the top plate off.
- Express the seam externally as a 1.5 mm shadow gap at Milestone 4. Do not
  try to hide it.

---

## 3. Mounting — no invented holes

**Printer.** Not screwed down. It drops into the well from above and is
captured by four tub walls at 2 mm clearance, sitting on two riser rails that
lift it 18 mm so its top lands flush with the top face. The rails leave a
cable channel underneath. Its own mounting holes stay unmeasured and unused.

**Display.** Not glued, and it does not use its own mounting holes either.
The panel sits against a 6 mm lip formed by the front wall, is located
laterally by two printed ribs, and is clamped from behind by
`display_retainer` into 4 bosses (2 low, 2 high — the only places with room).

**Lid clearance.** `printer_top_rim = 0`. The well is open across the whole
printer footprint with zero inward overhang, because the clamshell's hinge
line and swing arc are unmeasured. Stiffness comes from a 15 mm tall, 3 mm
thick flange on the *inside* of the walls instead of an inward lip. Do not
raise `printer_top_rim` until the lid is measured open.

---

## 4. Hardware

- 21 × M3 short heat-set insert (OD 4.6 × L 4.0) — hole Ø4.2, depth 6
- 21 × M3 socket head, 8–12 mm
- 2 × Ø4 × 12 steel dowel pin
- 4 × Ø14 adhesive rubber foot (8 recesses provided)

---

## 5. Print settings

- PETG or PLA, 0.4 nozzle, 0.2 layer, 4 perimeters, 25 % gyroid.
- Both tubs print open-top-up. No supports needed: the window and the rear
  opening are vertical, the flanges are vertical thickenings, the boss
  cylinders are horizontal-axis and self-supporting at Ø9.
- Tubs are ~18–24 h each. Print test coupons first (Milestone 2.5).

---

## 6. Assembly sequence

Validated as physically possible — no trapped fastener.

1. Heat-set all 21 inserts while the tubs are still empty.
2. Display into `tub_left` from behind → `display_retainer` → 4 × M3.
3. Pi and PSU into the left bay, cables routed to the spine pass-through.
4. Dowels into `tub_right`, mate the two tubs, 5 × M3 from the left bay.
5. Printer into the right well from above, cables down the riser channel.
6. `top_plate_left` → 4 × M3.
7. Rear panels → 8 × M3.
8. Feet.

Reverse for service. Paper roll replacement needs none of it: the lid is
exposed on the top face.

## 7. Revised milestones

**M0 — Metrology. Blocking. Nothing downstream is trustworthy until done.**
Caliper the printer: W, D, H of the *unit* (the 142/122/122 is almost
certainly the carton), the lid footprint, the lid's open swing, the paper slot
position, the LED and button positions on the front edge, all connector
positions. Caliper the display: total assembly thickness with the driver PCB
and standoffs, cable exit positions. Record the Pi model, cooler, PSU.
*Exit: every UNVERIFIED/TBD parameter in `lumon_v2.py` replaced with a
measured value.*

**M1 — Blockout.** Done. Re-run after M0 and re-check the envelope.

**M1.5 — Split and structure.** Done — this document.
*Exit: every part fits the bed, zero interference, assembly sequence valid.*

**M2 — Functional apertures.** Front slot for the printer LED and buttons,
rear port cutouts for power/USB/Ethernet, DSI routing with a generous radius,
vent slots on the rear and underside, cable channel under the riser.
*Blocked by M0 — all positions come from measurement.*

**M2.5 — Test coupons. New, and the highest-value hour in the plan.**
Print three fragments only: the seam joint corner (dowel + insert + screw),
the display rebate corner with a rib, and one riser rail end. ~1 h total.
Validates tolerances before committing 40 h of tub prints.
*Exit: dowel slides with hand pressure, insert seats square, display corner
holds the panel without rocking.*

**M3 — Mechanical refinement.** Ribbing where the coupons showed flex, cable
management features, fillet the internal corners, service-panel gasketing if
wanted.

**M4 — Industrial design.** Shadow gap at the seam, plinth line, display
bezel chamfer, recessed ID plate, single status LED. The aesthetic goes on
last, over a structure that already works.

**M5 — Printability pass.** Orientation, seam placement in the slicer, wall
count, first-layer adhesion on the 201 × 153 footprint.

**M6 — Prototype.** Print `tub_right` first: it carries the riskiest unknown
(the printer envelope). Fit the actual printer before printing `tub_left`.

**M7 — Revision.** Feed measured fit back into the parameter table and
regenerate.

## 8. Still unverified

Printer W/D/H, lid swing arc, LED/button positions, connector positions,
display assembly thickness, Pi model, PSU dimensions, cable types, and the
power architecture (one internal PSU with an IEC inlet, or two external
bricks — this decides the rear panel and the left bay volume).
