# Integral-front mechanical study

User preference: prioritize front robustness over convenient paper changes.

The front surround, side walls, reinforcing rail and floor are one solid and one grey print. There are no front-panel fasteners or removable front part. Top/rear covers, internal cradle tray and tray stop remain separate. To service paper when the native lid cannot open in place: remove the top cover, access/release cables and printer mounts through the rear, and lift the intact printer out. Do not dismantle the thermal-printer mechanism.

Five parts are exported. Geometry checks cover single solids, watertight meshes, pairwise interference, measured body clearance and a straight-up removal sweep with the top cover absent. Cables, unmodeled printer curves and the future retention bracket still require checks. The keyhole-mount measurements remain pending.

This is a mechanical study, not final industrial design or a print release. It retains a provisional large front opening until cutter/control positions are known. The housing's 201mm design height is 20mm taller than v19; shared silhouette, radii and logo layout remain unfinished. The blank block in assembly.png is the measured printer envelope. The integral front introduces a broad opening roof: slicing must verify orientation/supports before printing. No print was sent.

Source: cad/front_output_v20.py. Prior v20_front outputs are superseded.
